# Author's Note

This book uses direct language because the things in it are too hard to say softly.  That is why they are written.

The sequence of events may not be perfectly accurate.  The trip was ten days and it was a blur.  I did not think to record it at the time because I did not yet understand its impact.  I kept a daily blog while it was happening in real time, but that record has been lost.  What remains is memory, photographs, and the weight of what I carried home.  The events are real.  The people are real.  The order may shift because that is how memory works — it organizes by impact, not by calendar.  The things that hit the hardest are the clearest.  The rest I have reconstructed as honestly as I can.

It was our escape of the hard way that prepared us to face it again.

This book is not safe.  If that is difficult to read, it should be.  It was difficult to live.
