# Escaping the Hard Way: Knock and Plead

*A memoir by Andrew Gauger*

In November 2018, three American drug addicts surrendered themselves to a chief of police in the Philippines, confessed to their pasts, and said we heard you were killing drug addicts and thought you'd want to take us in.

He laughed.  He said he'd take whatever we had.  Then he took us to the basement.
